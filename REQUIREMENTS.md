# User Requirements

MVP: the user enters their financial picture (income, spending, net worth
and the funds they have available) and one goal with a cost and a target
date. The app shows how their money compares with the goal: the
probability of reaching it if they invest, and a verdict on whether it is
realistic. Results update automatically. How the probability is worked
out is described in SPECIFICATIONS.md.

## Financial Profile

1. As a user, I can enter my monthly income, my monthly spending, and
   optionally my net worth.
2. As a user, I can see how much money I have left over each month, and
   clearly see when I spend more than I earn.
3. As a user, I can see my savings rate: the share of my income I keep
   each month.

## Manage Funds

4. As a user, I can enter the funds I have available to put toward my
   goal, update them later, and see my results for the goal change with
   them.
5. As a user, I can record what I spend, so my available funds stay up
   to date and I can see how my spending affects my goal.

## Financial Goal

6. As a user, I can create one financial goal with a name, a target amount
   and a target date.
7. As a user, I can see how much I need to save each month to reach my
   goal by its date, and whether what I have left each month is enough.
8. As a user, I can see when I would reach my goal at my current pace, or
   how much more I would need to save each month to make it in time.

## Investment Portfolios

9. As a user, I can see the investment options my plan chooses from,
   from low risk to high risk, with how much each is expected to grow and
   how much it can rise and fall.

## Goal Probability and Verdict

10. As a user, I can see the probability of reaching my goal by its date
    if I invest, as a percentage.
11. As a user, I get a verdict on whether my goal is realistic: Realistic,
    Within reach, A stretch, or Not realistic yet.
12. As a user, I can see why I got that verdict: what I will have put
    away before any growth compared with the goal, and how much I would
    need to save each month without growth.
13. As a user, I can see which portfolio the plan uses each year until my
    goal, and what I would end up with in a bad, a typical and a good
    market.
14. As a user, I can see how the probability compares with a best-case
    check of the same plan, so I know how far I can trust it.

## Using the App

15. As a user, the results update automatically when I change my money or
    my goal, I am told what is wrong and how to fix it when I enter a value
    the app can't use, and I can clear everything to start over.
16. As a user, the same inputs always give me the same probability.
