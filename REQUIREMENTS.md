# User Requirements

Walking skeleton feature: Goal Probability Planner (single goal).
The user enters their financial picture and one goal, and the app shows
the probability of reaching that goal and whether it is realistic.

1. As a user, I can enter my financial picture: net worth, yearly income,
   yearly expenses, the amount I invest each month, and my age.
2. As a user, I can create one financial goal with a name, a target cost,
   and a target month and year.
3. As a user, I can see the estimated probability of reaching my goal in full.
4. As a user, I can see a verdict on whether my goal is realistic, and the
   probability and verdict update automatically when I change my picture
   or my goal.
