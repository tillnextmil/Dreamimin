# User Requirements

Walking skeleton feature: Financial Profile and One Goal (M1).
The user enters their monthly income, monthly spending and net worth, and
creates one savings goal. The app shows how much money is left each month,
how much the goal needs each month, and whether the user is on track to
reach it in time. The probability of reaching a goal, from our planning
algorithm, is added in M2 on top of these same inputs.

1. As a user, I can enter my monthly income, my monthly spending, and
   optionally my net worth.
2. As a user, I can see how much money I have left over each month, and
   clearly see when I spend more than I earn.
3. As a user, I can see my savings rate: the share of my income I keep
   each month.
4. As a user, I can create one financial goal with a name, a target amount
   and a target date.
5. As a user, I can see how much I need to save each month to reach my
   goal by its date, and whether what I have left each month is enough.
6. As a user, I can see when I would reach my goal at my current pace, or
   how much more I would need to save each month to make it in time.
7. As a user, the results update automatically when I change my money or
   my goal, I am told what is wrong and how to fix it when I enter a value
   the app can't use, and I can clear everything to start over.
